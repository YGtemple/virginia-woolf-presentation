她所处的时代：女性的“无形围墙”

Women's Invisible Walls
i.

No Right to Vote没有投票权

Women over 30 only gained the vote in 1918; equal suffrage came in 1928.

30岁以上女性1918年才获选举权，平等选举权1928年才实现。

ii.

Closed Professions职业大门紧闭

Law, medicine, the church and the academy were largely closed to women.

法律、医学、教会与学术界几乎不接纳女性。

iii.

The Angel Ideal“家庭天使”理想

Women were to be gentle and selfless; writing seemed “unwomanly.”

社会期待女性温顺无私，写作被视为“不像女人”。

Speech · Guide

演讲导览

Professions for Women

What It Is作品概述

“Professions for Women” is a speech Woolf gave to the National Society for Women's Service on 21 January 1931, later collected in The Death of the Moth.

《Professions for Women》是伍尔夫1931年1月21日在全国妇女服务协会发表的演讲，后收入随笔集《飞蛾之死》。

At a Glance关键信息

Date 时间
21 January 1931
Venue 场合
National Society for Women's Service
Theme 主题
Women, work and the freedom to write　女性、职业与写作的自由

With wit and candour, she reflects on what it cost a woman to become a professional writer — and looks forward to the rooms yet to be furnished.

她以幽默与坦率，讲述女性成为职业作家所付出的代价，并展望那些尚待布置的“房间”。

Part I

精讲一：写作这门职业

The Pioneers Who Cleared the Road

Woolf opens by noting that in literature, fewer “experiences peculiar to women” existed — because the road had been cut long before.

伍尔夫开场便说，在文学这一行，女性特有的经历反而更少——因为道路早被前人开辟出来。

She names Aphra Behn, Fanny Burney, Harriet Martineau, Jane Austen and George Eliot — women who smoothed the path and “regulated my steps.”

她列举 Aphra Behn、Fanny Burney、Harriet Martineau、Jane Austen、George Eliot——这些女性铺平了道路、“规范了我的步伐”。

Writing was cheap, private and “reputable and harmless”; it did not disturb the peace of the family.

写作成本低廉、可在家中私下进行，“体面而无害”，不会惊扰家庭的安宁。

Part II

精讲二：杀死“家中的天使”

Killing the Angel in the House

Behind her as she wrote stood the “Angel in the House,” from Coventry Patmore's 1854 poem — charming, sympathetic and utterly unselfish; above all, “she never had a mind or a wish of her own.”

写作时，她身后站着“家中的天使”（出自 Coventry Patmore 1854年的诗）：迷人、体贴、完全无私；最重要的是，“她从来没有自己的想法或愿望”。

When she tried to write the truth, the Angel whispered: be tender, flatter and deceive — hide what you really think.

当她想写出真实想法，天使便低语：要温柔、讨好、说假话——藏起你真正的念头。

The Angel was never a real person — she was every “good woman” society expected her to be, living inside her head; to kill her was to silence her inner censor.

这位天使并不存在，她是社会要求她做的每一个“好女人”，活在她自己脑中；杀死她，就是杀死自己脑中的审查者。

“I turned upon her and caught her by the throat. I did my best to kill her.”
“我转过身，一把掐住她的喉咙；我竭尽全力杀死了她。”——不杀死天使，天使就会杀死她心中的作家；这是女性作家职业的一部分。
Part III

精讲三：言说真实的困境

The Difficulty of Telling the Truth

After the Angel was dead, she hoped simply to “be herself” and write — yet being oneself proved far from simple.

天使死后，她以为可以“做自己”、安心写作——但“做自己”谈何容易。

She found it extraordinarily hard to “tell the truth about my own experiences as a body”; there was no ready language, and what a man could say freely would shock readers from a woman's pen.

她发现“如实讲述自己身体的经验”异常艰难：没有现成的语言，男作家能坦率书写的内容，出自女性之笔便会震惊读者。

The first wall was other people's eyes; the second was her own pen — there was no language a woman could safely use.

第一堵墙是别人的眼光，第二堵墙是自己的笔——当时没有一套女人能安全使用的语言。

The male-dominated professions still met women with prejudice and few precedents.

男性主导的职业世界，仍以偏见和缺乏先例迎接着女性。

Part IV

精讲四：“新房间”与未来

The New Rooms, Left to the Next Generation

Remember the “room of one's own”? Here she imagines professional life as a great house: writing was the first room she opened, while law, medicine and politics were still locked.

还记得前面那间“自己的房间”吗？这里她把职业想成一栋大宅：写作是她推开的第一扇门，法律、医学、政治的门还锁着。

The Great House职业如大宅

She imagines professional life as a great house with many rooms — some bright, some closed to her — which women were only beginning to enter.

她把职业生活想象成一栋有许多房间的大宅：有的明亮，有的对她紧闭，女性才刚刚开始走入。

Entrusted Forward托付给下一代

She admits she could not fully see those rooms or say how to furnish them; that task belonged to the next generation of women.

她坦言自己无法看遍那些房间、也无法回答该如何布置；那是属于下一代女性的任务。

Her closing words entrust the future — and the freedom to write and work — to the young women listening.

在结尾，她把未来、写作与工作的自由，郑重托付给在场的年轻女性。

The Art of the Speech

演讲的艺术与金句

Rhetoric and Memorable Lines

Craft and Voice艺术特色

Wit, irony and self-deprecation; powerful metaphors — the Angel and the house; a first-person, conversational voice; and a deliberate echo of A Room of One's Own.

幽默、反讽与自嘲；“天使”与“房子”的有力隐喻；第一人称的对话感；并与《一间自己的房间》遥相呼应。
